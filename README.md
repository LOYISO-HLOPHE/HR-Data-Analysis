# HR-Data-Analysis (Interactive Dashbord creation using Tablaeu and MS Excel)
## Project Objective
- Visualize the overall attrition rate and key HR metrics (Active Employees, Average Age).
- Identify which departments and job roles are experiencing the highest turnover.
- Analyze how age, gender, and education level influence retention.
- Evaluate job satisfaction levels across different roles to pinpoint potential problem areas.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/LOYISO-HLOPHE/HR-Data-Analysis/blob/main/HR%20Data.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Key Insight
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12% of employees left the organization.
- High-Risk Departments: The Sales Department has the highest attrition count (133 employees), representing over 56% of all departures.
- Age & Gender Trends: The 25–34 age group shows the highest attrition, with a significant split between genders (112 females vs. 69 males).
- Education Impact: Employees with a background in Life Sciences show the highest attrition numbers, followed closely by Medical and Marketing fields.
- Job Satisfaction Heatmap: Most departures are among employees with lower job satisfaction scores, though there are notable outliers in specific roles like Sales Executive and Research Scientist across different satisfaction levels.
